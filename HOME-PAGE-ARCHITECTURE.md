## Homepage Flow Summary

```
1. HERO     ─── "Technology that transforms. Trust that endures."
                  ↓
2. TRUST    ─── Stats + logos (credibility before anything else)
                  ↓
3. CAPABILITIES ─ "What we do" reframed as business outcomes (4 pillars)
                  ↓
4. SERVICES ─── Top 3 services with depth (ERP, E-Commerce, Connectivity)
                  ↓
5. MATGER   ─── Flagship product proof (builds, not just advises)
                  ↓
6. INDUSTRIES ─ "We understand your sector" (5 industries)
                  ↓
7. WHY TUTIA ── Differentiators (local, proven, end-to-end, bilingual)
                  ↓
8. METRICS  ─── Quantified proof (counters animate on scroll)
                  ↓
9. CASE STUDIES ─ Real projects, real results (3 previews)
                  ↓
10. TESTIMONIALS ─ Human voices, social proof
                  ↓
11. CRM FORM ─── Embedded conversion (low friction, on-page)
                  ↓
12. FINAL CTA ── "Book Consultation" / "Request Proposal"
```

**Conversion strategy**: Trust builds progressively. Each section addresses a specific barrier. The CRM form appears at section 11 — after the visitor has seen proof, but before they need to navigate away. The final CTA catches anyone who prefers a higher-touch entry point.

**Mobile flow**: Same 12 sections, stacked vertically. Each section is 1-2 viewports max. CRM form simplifies to 3 fields (Name, Phone, Message). All CTAs full-width on mobile.
